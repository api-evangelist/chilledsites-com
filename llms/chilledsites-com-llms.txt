## What is ChilledSites?

ChilledSites is an AI website builder. Users describe their business in plain English and get a production-ready, mobile-responsive website in under 60 seconds. Sites deploy instantly to .chilledsites.com subdomains or custom domains.

Powered by Claude Sonnet, GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5, and Grok 4. Used by 8,000+ businesses across 100+ countries — cafes, cleaners, photographers, startups, personal trainers, wedding planners, and service businesses.
